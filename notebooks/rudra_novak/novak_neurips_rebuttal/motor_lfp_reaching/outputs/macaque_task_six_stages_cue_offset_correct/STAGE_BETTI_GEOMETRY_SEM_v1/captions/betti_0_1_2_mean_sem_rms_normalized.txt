Mean and SEM of per-trial integrated Betti counts across six corrected task stages (198 trials per stage). For each cloud and homology dimension, integrate beta_h(distance) from 0 to 0.553385 and divide by that common interval length. The endpoint is the largest finite H0 death across all saved clouds and is the same for beta0, beta1, beta2 and all stages in this panel. Distance convention: rms_normalized. Y axis uses a zero-preserving symmetric log scale because the dimensions span orders of magnitude. Error bars are trial SEM, not a test of stage differences. All 1,188 saved topology clouds contribute.
